A wide, glanceable monitor of everything running, meant for a secondary display such as an ultrawide bar screen. It mirrors the main window and never drives it, apart from cancelling.

1. Click the Travel Display icon in the command bar, or pick **Open Travel Display** from ⌘K. It opens on a non-primary display (preferring a very wide one), or as a compact bar on your main screen if there is only one. The same command closes it.
2. Read the colony. Every run in flight is a Mochi, and each signal is literal:
   - **Zone** — the spec it belongs to. Chat and anything without a spec live in **Assistant & system**.
   - **Position** — live work roams the upper part of its zone; queued and blocked work sleeps in the lower right.
   - **Leash** — the dashed violet line runs to what it is waiting on (the header legend says **waiting on**).
   - **Visor light** — a scanning bar while working; a violet sideways glance for spec drafting, audits and reviews; shut with `zzz` while queued; `^ ^` when done; crossed out when failed.
   - **Speech bubble** — the tool it is running right now. A sleeping one says what it waits on; a failed one says **Failed — click to see why**.
3. Finished runs move to the **Recent** strip at the bottom.
4. Click a creature to open its full run detail, with the colony compressed beside it. Press `Esc` or **Back to the colony** to return.
5. The header has the task-slot meter, **Stop all**, zoom and theme. The wave icon is quiet mode: it freezes every animation in the colony.

## Tip

Quiet mode is on by default if your system asks for reduced motion, and the choice is remembered. Turn it on when the display sits in your peripheral vision — a drifting creature is exactly what it is designed to make you look at.
