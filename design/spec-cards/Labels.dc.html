The heading map

The whole narration layer is this table — a heading, lowercased, looks up a role and the connective that leads into it. No Claude run, no latency, nothing to cache or invalidate. Everything else on a beat (the counted line under the title) comes from the parsed section.

A heading the map doesn’t know still renders as a beat — dashed node, the neutral SECTION kicker, no connective, and the counted line if the content is countable. Nothing is dropped and nothing is invented. Adding a role later is one line in the table, not a migration.
